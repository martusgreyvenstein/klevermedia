Turning a Year of Impact Into a Story Worth Reading

Client: Kin Culture  ·  March 2026

Overview

Kin Culture came to us needing their annual report to do more than tick a compliance box — they wanted a document that people would actually read, and that reflected the year of work behind the numbers. We wrote and structured the report so the data, the milestones, and the people involved come together as a single, coherent story.

What we did

We worked closely with the Kin Culture team to gather the year's key facts and figures, then shaped that material into a clear narrative arc — from context and challenges through to outcomes — using the same story-driven approach we bring to all of our content work.

  • Structured the report around a clear narrative, not just a list of stats
  • Wrote all copy in Kin Culture's voice, for their specific audience
  • Worked from source data and interviews provided by the Kin Culture team
