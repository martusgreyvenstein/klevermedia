Overview

The Academy for Environmental Leadership works to develop the next generation of conservation and environmental leaders. We produce content pieces that help communicate their programmes clearly, to the students and partners deciding whether to get involved.

What we do

Environmental leadership programmes can be technical and mission-driven at the same time — our job is to keep that mission clear without losing the substance behind it, writing content that speaks to AEL's audience directly.

  • Content that explains AEL's programmes in plain, engaging language
  • Copy aligned with AEL's conservation and education mission
  • Ongoing content pieces produced as programmes evolve
