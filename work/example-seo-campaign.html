SEO / GEO

Scaling Organic Traffic Through Strategic SEO

Client: B2B Technology Brand  ·  February 2026

Overview

A B2B technology brand came to Klever Media with a clear problem: their website was invisible in organic search, and their competitors were capturing demand they should have been winning. Despite a strong product and an established customer base, their digital presence simply wasn't pulling its weight.

The challenge

The brand had a technically sound website but almost no content strategy underpinning it. They were targeting high-volume keywords they had no authority to rank for, while missing dozens of high-intent, lower-competition opportunities that their buyers were actually searching for. On top of this, with AI-generated search results becoming more common, they had zero presence in the answers their audience was receiving from tools like ChatGPT and Perplexity.

Our approach

We started with a full SEO audit — technical, on-page, and content — to establish a clear baseline and identify the highest-leverage opportunities. From there we developed a combined SEO and GEO strategy built around three pillars:

  • Technical fixes: crawlability improvements, Core Web Vitals optimisation, and structured data implementation
  • Content: a prioritised editorial plan targeting high-intent, mid-funnel search terms with genuine buying signal
  • GEO: restructuring key pages and adding authoritative, factual content designed to be cited by AI-generated answers

The results

Within six months the brand saw substantial gains across both traditional search and AI-powered discovery — with organic traffic tripling, multiple first-page rankings for target terms, and measurable brand mentions appearing in ChatGPT and Perplexity responses.
