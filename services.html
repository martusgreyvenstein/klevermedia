Common questions

What is SEO and why does my business need it?

SEO (Search Engine Optimisation) improves your website's visibility in organic search results. Businesses need SEO to attract qualified traffic, build credibility, and reduce long-term reliance on paid advertising.

What is GEO — Generative Engine Optimisation?

GEO is the practice of optimising your digital presence to appear in AI-generated answers from platforms like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews. As more people use AI to research and make decisions, GEO ensures your brand is part of those answers.

How does content marketing drive business growth?

Content marketing attracts and engages your target audience by providing genuinely useful, relevant content. Over time it builds brand authority, improves SEO performance, earns trust, and creates a compounding asset that continues driving traffic and leads long after publication.

Do you offer retainers or one-off projects?

Both. We offer ongoing retainer partnerships for brands that want continuous strategic support, and project-based engagements for defined scopes like audits, strategy development, or content campaigns. We'll recommend whichever fits your needs.
