Common questions

What's included in your strategy work?

Strategy is where everything starts — digital communications audits, audience research, channel and platform strategy, messaging and positioning, and KPI planning. It's the plan that connects your mission to the right channels, audiences, and messages before we produce anything.

What is Marketing Engineering?

Marketing Engineering is the technical backbone that makes your marketing actually work — SEO and GEO infrastructure, analytics and tracking setup, marketing automation, CRM integration, and website performance. It's the unglamorous, essential systems work most agencies skip past.

What kind of content do you produce?

We produce written content (articles, website copy, reports), ideation for new media like podcasts and social reels, writing and asset creation for AI search, and social media content and management — the full range needed to bring a brand's story to life, built on the strategy and systems we put in place first.

Do you offer retainers or one-off projects?

Both. We offer ongoing retainer partnerships for brands that want continuous strategic support, and project-based engagements for defined scopes like audits, strategy development, or content campaigns. We'll recommend whichever fits your needs.
